Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy explains how Marketing Scale ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you visit marketing-scale.com, fill in our form, or contact us. If you have questions, email info@marketing-scale.com.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request an audit or contact us, we collect what you enter: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, business type and message. We also keep the messages you send us by email, text or WhatsApp.

Information collected automatically. When you browse the site, we and our partners use cookies, pixels and similar technologies to collect things like pages viewed, clicks, device and browser type, approximate location (from IP address), the ad or link that brought you here, and how you move around the page.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to you, schedule and run your free audit, and provide our services.
  • To contact you by email, phone, text message or WhatsApp about your request. You can reply STOP to any text or ask us to stop at any time.
  • To measure site traffic and understand which pages and ads work.
  • To show our ads to people who visited the site and to measure ad performance (for example on Facebook and Instagram).
  • To keep the site secure and meet legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, for example:

  • Google (Workspace/Gmail, Google Sheets and Apps Script, where form submissions are stored; Analytics; Tag Manager)
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram advertising, WhatsApp messaging)
  • Microsoft (Clarity)
  • Our phone and text-messaging provider
  • GitHub and Cloudflare (website hosting and domain services)

Letting advertising partners like Meta collect data through their pixel may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some U.S. state privacy laws. You can opt out by clicking "Opt out" in the cookie notice, using the button above, or turning on Global Privacy Control. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not share your phone number or text-messaging consent with third parties for their own marketing.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your contact and form details for as long as we are in contact or working together, and generally for no more than 3 years after our last contact, unless the law requires longer. Analytics and advertising data are kept according to each provider's retention settings.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to withdraw consent. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you may also object to or restrict processing and complain to your local data protection authority. We rely on your consent for cookies and on our legitimate interest and your request for replying to inquiries.

To make a request, email info@marketing-scale.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

7. International transfers

We work with clients in the United States, Israel and Europe, and our service providers may store data in the United States and other countries. When we transfer data, we rely on our providers' standard safeguards.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their information.
